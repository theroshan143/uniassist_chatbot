### Placement Process
1. **Pre-Placement Talks (PPTs)**: Companies visit campus to present their profile and roles. Attendance is mandatory for interested students.
2. **Registration**: Register for each company via the Placement Portal (**placement.sit.edu.in**).
3. **Selection Rounds**: Typically includes Online Test → Group Discussion → Technical Interview → HR Interview.
4. **Offer Letter**: Companies submit offer letters to TPO. Students accept/reject within **3 working days**.
5. **Dream Company Policy**: Students placed in a company offering < ₹8,00,000 CTC can sit for "dream" companies offering ≥ ₹8,00,000.

### Eligibility for Placement
- No active backlogs at the time of the placement drive.
- Minimum **CGPA of 6.0** (some companies may require higher).
- 75% attendance in the current semester.
- Must have completed all mandatory training modules (aptitude, soft skills, mock interviews).

## Internships

### Mandatory Internship
- All B.Tech students must complete a **minimum 8-week internship** after 6th semester (summer of 3rd year).
- Internship carries **4 credits** and is graded based on a report + presentation.

### Internship Support
- TPO shares internship listings from partner companies starting **January** each year.
- Students can also self-source internships with approval from the **Faculty Internship Coordinator**.
- Stipend range (campus-sourced): **₹10,000 – ₹50,000 per month**.

### Internship Evaluation
- Submit an **Internship Report** (5,000–8,000 words) to the department within **2 weeks** of completion.
- Present findings to a panel of 2 faculty members.
- Company supervisor must submit a **feedback form** (sent by TPO via email).

## Training Programs
The TPO conducts the following training programs for pre-final and final year students:
- **Aptitude Training**: Quantitative, Verbal, Logical – 40 hours (August–October)
- **Soft Skills & Communication**: Group discussion, email etiquette, presentation – 20 hours
- **Technical Mock Interviews**: DSA, DBMS, OS, CN – conducted by industry mentors
- **Resume Building Workshop**: October each year
- **LinkedIn & Professional Branding**: Webinar in September

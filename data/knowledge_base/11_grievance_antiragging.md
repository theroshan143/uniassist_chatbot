# Student Grievance, Anti-Ragging, and Counselling – Sample Institute of Technology (SIT)

## Student Grievance Redressal

### Grievance Cell
SIT has a dedicated **Student Grievance Redressal Cell (SGRC)** to address academic, administrative, and personal grievances of students.

### How to File a Grievance
1. **Online**: Submit a grievance via the SIT Student Portal → "Grievance" tab. Attach supporting documents if any.
2. **Written**: Submit a written complaint to the **SGRC Chairperson** at Room 112, Administrative Block.
3. **In Person**: Walk-in during grievance cell office hours.

### Grievance Resolution Process
- Acknowledgement within **2 working days** of filing.
- Investigation and initial response within **7 working days**.
- If unresolved, escalation to the **Principal** within **14 working days**.
- Final appeal to the **Governing Body** if the student is unsatisfied with the resolution.

- **Chairperson**: Prof. Dr. Kavitha Rao (Dean, Student Welfare)
- **Members**: One faculty from each department, 2 student representatives, 1 administrative staff.
- **Email**: grievance@sit.edu.in

- **Office Hours**: Monday, Wednesday, Friday – 2:00 PM – 5:00 PM

## Anti-Ragging Policy

SIT has a **zero-tolerance policy towards ragging** in compliance with UGC Regulations and the Supreme Court of India directives.

### What Constitutes Ragging
- Any act of physical or mental abuse, humiliation, or harassment of a junior student by a senior.
- Forcing a student to perform acts against their will, using abusive language, bullying (including cyber-bullying).
- Any act that causes fear, shame, or distress.

### Anti-Ragging Committee
- **Chairperson**: Prof. Dr. Suresh Menon (Dean, Student Welfare)
- **Members**: 5 faculty members, 2 non-teaching staff, 3 senior students, 1 representative from civil administration, 1 NGO representative.
- **Anti-Ragging Squad**: Conducts surprise inspections of hostels, canteens, and common areas.

### How to Report Ragging
- **Toll-Free Helpline**: **1800-180-5522** (UGC National Anti-Ragging Helpline)
- **SIT Ragging Complaint**: Email to **antiragging@sit.edu.in** or call **+91-80-2345-6715**.
- **Anonymous Reporting**: Drop a written complaint in the sealed **Anti-Ragging Boxes** located at:
  - Main entrance of each hostel block
  - Academic Block-A entrance
  - Library entrance
  - Canteen entrance
- **Online**: portal.sit.edu.in → "Anti-Ragging Complaint" (can be anonymous).

### Punishments for Ragging
| Severity | Punishment |
|----------|-----------|
| Verbal abuse/mild ragging | Warning + counselling + apology letter + inform parents |
| Repeated or moderate ragging | Suspension from classes for 1–4 weeks + hostel expulsion |
| Severe ragging (physical harm, forcing acts) | Expulsion from SIT + FIR filed with police |
| Cyber-bullying/online ragging | Suspension + cyber cell involvement |

### Anti-Ragging Undertaking
- Every student and parent/guardian must submit a signed **Anti-Ragging Undertaking** at the time of admission.
- Available at: antiragging.in/affidavit

# Admissions and Eligibility – Sample Institute of Technology (SIT)

## Undergraduate Admissions (B.Tech)

### Eligibility Criteria
- Candidates must have passed Class 12 (or equivalent) from a recognized board with **Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM)**.
- Minimum aggregate in PCM: **60%** (General category), **55%** (OBC), **50%** (SC/ST/PwD).
- Valid score in **JEE Main** or **SIT Entrance Test (SIT-ET)** conducted in May each year.
- Age limit: Candidates must be born on or after **1st October 2006** (for 2026 admissions).

### Available B.Tech Programs
| Program | Intake | JEE Code |
|---------|--------|----------|
| Computer Science & Engineering | 180 | SIT-CSE |
| Artificial Intelligence & Data Science | 120 | SIT-AIDS |
| Electronics & Communication | 120 | SIT-ECE |
| Mechanical Engineering | 60 | SIT-ME |
| Civil Engineering | 60 | SIT-CE |
| Information Technology | 120 | SIT-IT |

### Admission Process
1. Register on the SIT Admissions Portal at **admissions.sit.edu.in** (opens **1st March** each year).
2. Fill the application form and upload documents (Class 10 & 12 mark sheets, ID proof, photograph, signature).
3. Pay the application fee: **₹1,200** (General/OBC) or **₹600** (SC/ST/PwD) via online payment.
4. Merit list published on **15th June** based on JEE Main rank (70% weightage) + Class 12 PCM percentage (30% weightage).
5. Counselling rounds: **Round 1 – 20th June**, **Round 2 – 1st July**, **Spot Round – 10th July**.
6. Report to campus with original documents by the date mentioned in the allotment letter.

### Documents Required at Reporting
- Class 10 and 12 mark sheets and certificates (original + 2 photocopies)
- JEE Main scorecard or SIT-ET admit card + scorecard
- Transfer Certificate and Migration Certificate
- Category certificate (if applicable)
- Aadhaar card
- 6 passport-size photographs
- Medical fitness certificate

## Postgraduate Admissions (M.Tech)
- Eligibility: B.Tech/B.E. with **60% aggregate** and a valid **GATE score**.
- Programs offered: M.Tech in CSE, AI & ML, VLSI Design, Structural Engineering.
- Intake: 30 seats per program.
- Application period: **1st April – 31st May**.
- Application fee: **₹1,500**.

## Lateral Entry (B.Tech 2nd Year)
- Diploma holders with **60% aggregate** can apply for direct entry into the 2nd year of B.Tech.
- Seats: 10% of sanctioned intake per program.
- Separate entrance test held in **July** each year.

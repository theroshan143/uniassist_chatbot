# Library Timings and Borrowing Rules – Sample Institute of Technology (SIT)

## Central Library – Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Knowledge Centre

### Location and Hours
- **Location**: Central Library Building (next to Academic Block-B)
- **Floors**: Ground + 2 floors, total area **3,500 sq. meters**
- **Seating Capacity**: 450 seats (including 50 in the silent study zone)

| Day | Timing |
|-----|--------|
| Monday – Friday | 8:00 AM – 10:00 PM |
| Saturday | 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM |
| Sunday & Holidays | 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM |
| During Exam Period | 7:00 AM – 11:00 PM (extended hours) |

### Collections
- **Books**: 85,000+ volumes covering engineering, science, humanities, and management.
- **Journals**: Subscriptions to 120+ national and international journals.
- **E-Resources**: Access to IEEE Xplore, Springer, Elsevier ScienceDirect, ACM Digital Library, NPTEL videos via campus network.
- **Project Reports**: Archive of 5,000+ B.Tech and M.Tech project reports.
- **Magazines & Newspapers**: 15 daily newspapers and 25 magazines.

## Borrowing Rules

### Borrowing Limits
| Member Type | Books | Duration | Renewals |
|-------------|-------|----------|----------|
| B.Tech Students | 4 books | 14 days | 1 renewal (14 days) |
| M.Tech Students | 6 books | 21 days | 2 renewals (14 days each) |
| PhD Scholars | 8 books | 30 days | 2 renewals (30 days each) |
| Faculty | 15 books | 90 days | Unlimited renewals |

### Late Return Fines
| Delay | Fine |
|-------|------|
| 1–7 days | ₹2 per day per book |
| 8–30 days | ₹5 per day per book |
| Beyond 30 days | ₹10 per day per book + borrowing privileges suspended |

### Lost or Damaged Books
- **Lost book**: Pay **twice the current market price** of the book, or replace with the same edition.
- **Damaged book**: Fine of **₹200 – ₹1,000** depending on extent of damage, assessed by the librarian.

## Library Services
1. **Book Reservation**: Reserve a currently issued book via the **Online Public Access Catalogue (OPAC)** at library.sit.edu.in. You'll be notified via email when the book is available.
2. **Inter-Library Loan**: Request books from partner libraries (IISc, IIIT-B) – processing time **5–7 working days**.
3. **Photocopying**: Self-service photocopier available on Ground Floor. ₹1 per page (A4), ₹2 per page (A3).
4. **Printing**: Laser printing at ₹3 per page (B&W), ₹10 per page (colour). Print station on 1st Floor.
5. **Book Bank**: SC/ST students can borrow textbooks for the entire semester under the **Book Bank Scheme**. Apply at the library counter with category certificate.
6. **Digital Library Zone**: 40 computers with internet access for e-resource browsing. 1st Floor, open during library hours.
7. **Discussion Rooms**: 4 soundproof discussion rooms (capacity 6–8) can be booked at the counter for group study. Maximum 2 hours per booking.

## Rules of Conduct
- Maintain **silence** in the reading areas. Use discussion rooms for group work.
- **Food and beverages** are not allowed inside the library.
- Personal bags must be kept in the **locker area** at the entrance (bring your own lock or buy one for ₹50).
- Mobile phones must be on **silent mode**.

# Course Registration and Credit Rules – Sample Institute of Technology (SIT)

## Credit System Overview
SIT follows a **Choice-Based Credit System (CBCS)**. Each course carries credits based on contact hours:
- **1 credit** = 1 lecture hour/week OR 2 lab hours/week for the full semester.
- B.Tech requires a minimum of **160 credits** for graduation.
- M.Tech requires **64 credits** for graduation.

## Registration Process
1. Course registration opens on the SIT Student Portal (**portal.sit.edu.in**) one week before each semester begins.
2. **Pre-registration**: Select courses during the registration window (dates in Academic Calendar).
3. **Advisor Approval**: Each student is assigned a **Faculty Advisor** who must approve the course selection within **3 working days**.
4. **Confirmation**: Pay semester fees to confirm registration. Unpaid registrations are cancelled.
5. **Late Registration**: Allowed up to **1 week after classes begin** with a late fee of **₹1,000**.

## Course Drop and Withdrawal
- **Course Drop (without penalty)**: Within the first **3 weeks** of the semester. No record on transcript.
- **Course Withdrawal (with W grade)**: Between week 4 and week 10. A "W" (Withdrawn) grade appears on the transcript but does not affect CGPA.
- **After week 10**: No withdrawal allowed. Student must appear for the exam.
- Maximum **2 drops** and **2 withdrawals** allowed per semester.

## Backlog and Repeat Courses
- Students with backlogs must register for the failed course in the **next available semester**.
- Backlog courses have priority over electives during registration.
- Maximum backlog limit: **8 subjects**. Students exceeding this limit are **debarred from registering for new courses** until backlogs are cleared.

## Credit Transfer
- Credits from NPTEL/SWAYAM courses (with certificate) can substitute **up to 2 open electives** per semester.
- Credits from student exchange programs at partner universities are transferable with Dean's approval.

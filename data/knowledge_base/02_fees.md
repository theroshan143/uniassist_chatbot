### One-Time Fees (At Admission Only)
- Caution Deposit (refundable): **₹10,000**
- Admission Processing Fee: **₹5,000**
- Identity Card & Library Card: **₹500**

## M.Tech Fee Structure (Per Semester)
- Tuition: **₹45,000** | Development: **₹10,000** | Lab: **₹8,000** | Total: **₹68,000**
- GATE-qualified students receive a monthly stipend of **₹12,400** as per AICTE norms.

## Payment Deadlines
- **Odd Semester (August)**: Fee must be paid by **31st July** each year.
- **Even Semester (January)**: Fee must be paid by **31st December** each year.

### Late Fee Penalties
| Delay | Penalty |
|-------|---------|
| 1–15 days late | ₹500 |
| 16–30 days late | ₹1,500 |
| 31–45 days late | ₹3,000 |
| Beyond 45 days | Name struck off rolls; re-admission required with ₹5,000 re-admission fee |

- **Demand Draft**: In favour of "Sample Institute of Technology", payable at Bengaluru. Submit at Accounts Office, Room 105, Administrative Block.
- **Bank Transfer (NEFT/RTGS)**: Account Name: Sample Institute of Technology, Bank: State Bank of India, A/c No: 3842 1067 9520, IFSC: SBIN0001234, Branch: SIT Campus Branch.

## Scholarships and Fee Waivers
1. **SIT Merit Scholarship**: Top 5% of each branch by CGPA – **50% tuition waiver**.
2. **Need-Based Financial Aid**: Family income below **₹5,00,000/year** – up to **75% tuition waiver**. Apply via the Dean (Student Welfare) office with income certificate by **15th August**.
3. **SC/ST Scholarship**: Full tuition waiver under state government scheme. Submit caste certificate and income certificate.
4. **Sports Scholarship**: National/State level athletes – **25% tuition waiver**. Submit certificates to Sports Officer.
5. **Sibling Discount**: Second sibling studying at SIT simultaneously – **10% tuition discount**.

## Refund Policy
- Withdrawal before classes begin: **90% refund** (processing fee non-refundable).
- Within 15 days of classes starting: **80% refund**.
- 16–30 days: **50% refund**.
- After 30 days: **No refund**.

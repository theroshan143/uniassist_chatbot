# Campus Facilities – Sample Institute of Technology (SIT)

## Campus Overview
SIT is spread over a **50-acre campus** located in Whitefield, Bengaluru, Karnataka. The campus is well-equipped with modern infrastructure and amenities.

## Transportation

### College Bus Service
- SIT operates **15 bus routes** covering major areas of Bengaluru.
- Routes include: Majestic, Koramangala, Jayanagar, Indiranagar, Hebbal, Yelahanka, Electronic City, HSR Layout, Marathahalli, Banashankari, and 5 more.
- **Bus fee**: ₹12,000 per semester (can be paid with tuition fees).
- **Timings**: Buses depart from pick-up points at **7:30 AM** and leave campus at **5:30 PM** and **7:00 PM**.
- Bus passes issued at the Transport Office, Ground Floor, Administrative Block.

### Parking
- **Student two-wheeler parking**: Free, at designated zones near each academic block.
- **Student car parking**: ₹2,000 per semester. Limited slots (apply at Transport Office).
- **Cycling encouraged**: Free bicycle stands near all blocks. SIT Cycle Share program: ₹500/semester for shared bicycles.

## Canteen and Food Courts

### Main Canteen – "Annapurna"
- **Location**: Near the Central Library
- **Timing**: 7:30 AM – 9:00 PM (Monday–Saturday), 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM (Sunday)
- **Cuisine**: South Indian, North Indian, Chinese, and snacks. Both veg and non-veg options.
- **Average meal cost**: ₹50–₹100

### Food Court – "Flavours"
- **Location**: Near the Sports Complex
- **Outlets**: Chai Point, Domino's, Subway, Fresh Juice Bar, South Indian Corner
- **Timing**: 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM

### Nescafé Kiosk
- **Locations**: Academic Block-A lobby and Library building entrance
- **Timing**: 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM

## Wi-Fi and Internet
- Entire campus covered by **high-speed Wi-Fi** (500 Mbps backbone).
- Network name: **SIT-Campus** (authenticated via SIT ID login).
- Each student gets **5 GB/day** data limit; unlimited during **11:00 PM – 6:00 AM**.
- **Eduroam** available for visiting researchers.
- Wired LAN available in hostel rooms (100 Mbps per port).
- IT Support for connectivity issues: **ithelpdesk@sit.edu.in** or call ext. **6720**.

## Grading System
SIT follows a **10-point CGPA** grading system:

| Grade | Grade Points | Marks Range |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| O (Outstanding) | 10 | 90–100 |
| A+ (Excellent) | 9 | 80–89 |
| A (Very Good) | 8 | 70–79 |
| B+ (Good) | 7 | 60–69 |
| B (Above Average) | 6 | 50–59 |
| C (Average) | 5 | 45–49 |
| P (Pass) | 4 | 40–44 |
| F (Fail) | 0 | Below 40 |

- Minimum **CGPA of 5.0** required for degree completion.
- Students with CGPA below **4.5** at the end of any academic year are placed on **Academic Probation**.

## Examination Rules
1. Students must carry their **SIT ID card** to every examination. No ID = no entry.
2. Arrive at least **15 minutes** before the scheduled start time.
3. No candidate will be admitted **30 minutes after** the exam starts.
4. Electronic devices (phones, smartwatches, earbuds) are **strictly prohibited**. Violation results in **cancellation of the paper**.
5. Use only **blue or black ink** pens. Pencils allowed for diagrams only.
6. Unfair means (copying, cheat sheets, impersonation) → **zero marks + disciplinary action** as per SIT Ordinance 7.
7. Answer booklets must not be torn or taken out of the hall.

## Re-Evaluation and Supplementary Exams
- **Re-evaluation**: Apply within **10 days** of result declaration. Fee: **₹500 per paper**. Apply at Examination Office or online at portal.sit.edu.in.
- **Supplementary Exam**: Held in **July** (for odd semester) and **September** (for even semester). Fee: **₹1,000 per paper**.
- Maximum **3 attempts** allowed per subject (including the first attempt).
- Students who fail in more than **4 subjects** in a semester must **repeat the semester**.

## Grade Improvement
- Students can re-appear in up to **2 subjects per semester** to improve grades, within one year of passing.
- Only the **higher grade** is retained.
- Fee: **₹1,500 per subject**.

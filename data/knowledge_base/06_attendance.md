# Attendance Policy – Sample Institute of Technology (SIT)

## Minimum Attendance Requirement
- Students must maintain a **minimum of 75% attendance** in each course (theory and lab separately).
- Attendance is recorded by the faculty at the start of each class using the biometric attendance system and verified via the SIT Student Portal.

## Consequences of Low Attendance
| Attendance | Consequence |
|------------|-------------|
| 74%–65% | **Warning letter** sent to student and parent/guardian |
| 64%–55% | **Detained from End Semester Exam** for that course; must appear in supplementary exam |
| Below 55% | **Deregistered from the course**; must re-register and repeat the course next year |

## Attendance Tracking
- Attendance is updated on the SIT Student Portal (**portal.sit.edu.in**) within **48 hours** of each class.
- Students can check their attendance anytime under **"My Attendance"** on the portal.
- **Weekly SMS alerts** are sent to registered parent/guardian mobile numbers for students below 80% attendance.

## Medical Leave
- Students with **genuine medical emergencies** can apply for medical leave.
- Submit a **medical certificate** from a registered medical practitioner to the Head of Department within **7 days** of resuming classes.
- Medical leave can compensate up to **15% additional attendance** (i.e., effective minimum becomes 60%).
- Maximum medical leave: **15 working days per semester**. Beyond this, apply to the Dean (Student Welfare) with supporting hospital records.
- **Hospitalization cases**: Direct admission to hospital or SIT Health Centre entitles automatic medical leave with health centre verification.

## On-Duty (OD) Leave
- Students representing SIT in official events (sports, technical fests, workshops, NCC/NSS) are marked **"On Duty"**.
- OD leave counts as **present** for attendance purposes.
- Prior approval required from the **Head of Department** and the **faculty coordinator** of the event.
- Maximum **10 OD days** per semester.

## Special Provisions
- Students with **disabilities (PwD)** may request relaxed attendance norms (minimum 65%) through the Dean (Student Welfare).
- **Pregnant students** may apply for up to **60 days of maternity leave** under UGC guidelines.
- Students undergoing **counselling treatment** at the SIT Wellness Centre may receive up to 5 additional leave days per semester with counsellor's recommendation.

## Re-admission After Detention
- Students detained from ESE due to attendance shortage must apply for **supplementary exam** and pay a fee of **₹1,000 per paper**.
- Attendance of the detained semester carries forward (i.e., the student need not re-attend classes if supplementary exam is available).

# Hostel Rules and Allotment – Sample Institute of Technology (SIT)

## Hostel Facilities
SIT provides on-campus hostel accommodation for **1,200 students** across 4 hostel blocks:

| Block | Type | Capacity | For |
|-------|------|----------|-----|
| Block A – Vivekananda Hostel | Boys | 400 | 1st & 2nd Year Boys |
| Block B – Raman Hostel | Boys | 300 | 3rd & 4th Year Boys |
| Block C – Kalpana Hostel | Girls | 350 | All Year Girls |
| Block D – Sarabhai Hostel | Co-ed PG | 150 | M.Tech & PhD |

## Room Types and Fees (Per Semester)
| Room Type | Fee | Availability |
|-----------|-----|-------------|
| Triple Sharing | ₹22,000 | All blocks |
| Double Sharing | ₹32,000 | Blocks B, C, D |
| Single Occupancy | ₹48,000 | Block D only (PG students) |

**Mess Fee** (compulsory for hostellers): **₹18,000 per semester** (covers breakfast, lunch, snacks, dinner).

## Allotment Process
1. Apply online via the SIT Student Portal → "Hostel Application" by **15th July** (odd semester) or **15th December** (even semester).
2. Allotment priority: (a) Outstation students (>100 km from campus), (b) Previous hostellers in good standing, (c) CGPA-based merit.
3. Allotment results published by **25th July** / **25th December**.
4. Pay hostel + mess fees within **7 days** of allotment to confirm.
5. Room keys and allotment slip collected from the **Hostel Office, Ground Floor, Block A**.

## Hostel Rules
1. **Entry/Exit**: Main gate closes at **10:00 PM**. Late entry (up to 11:00 PM) allowed with **prior permission from the Warden**. No entry after 11:00 PM except emergencies.
2. **Visitors**: Visiting hours are **4:00 PM – 7:00 PM** on weekdays and **10:00 AM – 7:00 PM** on weekends. Visitors must register at the hostel reception. **Opposite-gender visitors are not allowed inside rooms**.
3. **Alcohol, Smoking, and Drugs**: **Strictly prohibited**. Violation leads to immediate **expulsion from hostel** and disciplinary action.
4. **Noise**: Maintain silence between **10:30 PM – 6:00 AM** (study hours). Music speakers are not allowed.
5. **Electrical Appliances**: Only laptops, phone chargers, table lamps, and electric kettles (up to 1kW) are allowed. Room heaters, induction cooktops, and irons are **prohibited** (use common utility room).
6. **Room Maintenance**: Rooms are inspected monthly by the hostel committee. Students must keep rooms clean. **₹500 fine** for unhygienic conditions.
7. **Damage**: Any damage to hostel property is charged to the student. Cost deducted from the caution deposit.
8. **Leave**: For overnight absence, fill the **Leave Form** on the portal and get Warden's approval. Inform parents/guardians.

## Mess Committee
- A **Mess Committee** of 8 elected student representatives oversees food quality and menu planning.
- Menu rotates weekly and includes vegetarian and non-vegetarian options.
- Complaints can be filed via the **Mess Feedback Box** in each dining hall or online at portal.sit.edu.in → "Hostel Feedback".
- Special dietary requirements (Jain, vegan, medical diet) can be accommodated with a written request to the Mess Manager.
